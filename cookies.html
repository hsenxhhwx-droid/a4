Digital Governance

Cookie Policy & Storage Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • SparkStudyVale Privacy Protocol

1. Principles of Digital Restraint and Data Transparency

SparkStudyVale operates under a core commitment to digital minimalism, academic focus, and complete user privacy. We believe your learning journey through our cognitive education platform should be private, secure, and free from intrusive commercial advertising networks. This Cookie Policy explains clearly how small data files (cookies) and browser local storage are used across our website.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Session Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the core operational functioning and security of our digital academy. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session handshakes, balance server network loads across geographic regions, prevent cross-site request forgery, and support secure admission inquiry form transmissions. Without these necessary cookies, basic website security and interactive study tools cannot function.

3. Local Storage for Study Theme and Font Preferences

To optimize your cognitive reading comfort, SparkStudyVale utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your preferred lighting theme (Oxford Midnight, Campus Daylight, or Deep Neural Focus) and your in-article typography scale. This data remains stored exclusively within your local browser sandbox and is never shared with external tracking networks.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We employ Google Analytics 4 (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to gather privacy-compliant, anonymized telemetry regarding website traffic, reading durations, and technical device viewports. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of capture, guaranteeing that no personal identity or student record is ever linked to your browsing habits.

5. Browser Management and Opt-Out Controls

You retain complete control to inspect, block, or delete cookies at any time via your browser preferences menu (accessible in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain interactive features such as the memory retention calculator or digital admissions submission forms.

6. Governance Inquiries and Contact Details

If you have questions regarding our cookie practices or wish to review your digital privacy preferences, please reach our data privacy desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact us by phone at +1-888-777-5845.
