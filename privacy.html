Privacy Policy & Student Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • SparkStudyVale Academic Standards

1. Commitment to Student Privacy and Data Stewardship

At SparkStudyVale, we recognize that learning data, study habit metrics, and diagnostic assessment performance represent deeply personal cognitive records. This Privacy Policy details the exact operational standards, cryptographic protocols, and data governance practices governing how we collect, process, and safeguard your personal information when you access our digital academy, use our memory retention simulator, or enroll in our neuro-pedagogy cohorts.

2. Categories of Information Gathered Across Our Academic Platform

When you register for our courses, request academic advisory consultations, or utilize our spaced repetition diagnostic tools, we collect standard identifying information including your full name, email address, institutional affiliation, and academic focus areas. Additionally, to optimize spacing intervals, our platform records anonymized study performance telemetry (such as flashcard review latencies, recall accuracy scores, and interval spacing preferences). System telemetry including IP addresses and browser types is gathered via secure session cookies to ensure stable cross-platform rendering.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Educational Data Processing

We process student data strictly to fulfill educational services, deliver personalized spaced repetition scheduling, track cohort progress, and provide academic support. Anonymized study metrics are analyzed under legitimate interest frameworks to refine our cognitive retention algorithms and publish peer-reviewed pedagogical research. We enforce strict data minimization standards, ensuring that no unnecessary personal data is ever requested or retained.

4. Absolute Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Service Provider Governance

SparkStudyVale maintains a strict, unyielding policy: we never sell, rent, monetize, or exchange your personal information, diagnostic test scores, or study records with third-party marketing brokers or commercial advertisers. We share necessary operational data exclusively with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers, including encrypted payment gateways and cloud hosting servers compliant with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 data security standards. All partners are bound by strict non-disclosure covenants.

5. Cryptographic Security Standards & Retention Schedules

All digital transmissions on SparkStudyVale are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols, encrypted database storage, and multi-factor authentication controls. We retain student records only for the duration necessary to satisfy enrollment agreements, provide ongoing alumni learning access, and fulfill legal compliance mandates. When student accounts reach the end of their retention lifecycle, all associated personal data is permanently and securely erased.

6. International Privacy Rights & Data Protection Officer Contact

In accordance with GDPR, FERPA, CCPA, and international student privacy frameworks, you maintain complete rights to access, inspect, export, correct, or request the permanent deletion of your personal records from our databases. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our student data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Protection Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone +1-888-777-5845.
